### Game Details & Guide
Game Overview


Rooftop Snipers is a popular browser game that challenges players to become expert snipers, taking aim at various targets on a rooftop landscape. The objective of the game is simple: click the mouse button to shoot at targets, while avoiding obstacles and rival snipers.

In Rooftop Snipers, players are dropped onto a single-screen map with several rooftops, each containing a range of different targets. The game features various environments, including buildings, trees, and vehicles, which can be destroyed or used as cover. Players must navigate the landscape, using their aim to take down enemies and complete objectives.

The gameplay is divided into rounds, with players earning points for killing opponents, destroying vehicles, and collecting resources. As players progress through the game, new challenges and targets become available, requiring them to adapt their tactics and improve their aim.


How to Play


To play Rooftop Snipers, follow these steps:
• The game will automatically start a round when you launch it.
• Use your mouse to click on targets on the map. Each target has a point value associated with it.
• To move around the map, use the arrow keys or WASD keys.
• You can also use the zoom feature to get a closer look at certain areas of the map.
• If you're low on health, you'll lose all your progress and have to start from the beginning.


Tips and Tricks for Success

• Stay mobile: Constantly move around the map to avoid enemy fire and get a better view of your surroundings.
• Use obstacles to your advantage: Don't be afraid to destroy enemies or use environmental objects like vehicles as cover.
• Focus on high-value targets: Killing opponents with higher point values will give you more points at the end of the round.
• Learn the map: Familiarize yourself with the layout of the map, including hiding spots and common routes taken by enemy snipers.
